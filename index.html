How we prepare every car

Most complaints in this trade come from cars sold before they were ready. We'd rather hold a car back a few days than hand over a problem.

Step 1

Sourced & checked

History, mileage and condition verified before we buy. If it doesn't check out, we don't take it.

Step 2

Mechanical work

Servicing, MOT and any mechanical or bodywork repairs completed and recorded.

Step 3

Full inspection

A point-by-point check covering tyres, brakes, lights, electrics, fluids and interior.

Step 4

Cleaned & listed

Valeted, photographed and advertised — only once everything above is signed off.
